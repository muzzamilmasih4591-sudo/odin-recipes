Homemade Margherita Pizza

Description

This classic homemade Margherita pizza features a crispy golden crust topped with tangy tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil.

It takes just a few simple ingredients and bakes quickly in a hot oven for a restaurant-style dinner at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 ball store-bought or homemade pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato pizza sauce
  • 8 ounces fresh mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C) and place a baking sheet inside to heat up.
  2. Stretch or roll the pizza dough into a 12-inch circle on a piece of parchment paper.
  3. Brush the dough lightly with olive oil, then spread the tomato sauce evenly over the top.
  4. Arrange the mozzarella slices and sprinkle Parmesan cheese and salt over the sauce.
  5. Transfer the pizza onto the hot baking sheet and bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
  6. Top with fresh basil leaves, slice, and serve warm.
