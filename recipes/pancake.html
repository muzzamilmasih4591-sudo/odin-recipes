Fluffy Pancakes

Description

These classic fluffy pancakes are light, golden brown, and super easy to whip up from scratch for breakfast.

Serve them warm with butter, maple syrup, or fresh berries for a quick weekend treat.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  2. Make a well in the center and pour in the milk, egg, and melted butter; mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled frying pan or griddle over medium-high heat.
  4. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter onto the pan for each pancake.
  5. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown on the other side.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
